Our Protocol

Every patient at AyurRemedy goes through a holistic health evaluation before any treatment is planned.

Holistic health evaluation

  • At AyurRemedy, the patient is taken through a detailed evaluation of their present physical, mental, emotional, nutritional and social wellbeing and present lifestyle.
  • A thorough head-to-toe clinical examination uncovers health risk factors the patient may be unaware of, whether or not they are connected with the presenting complaint.
  • A complete picture of the person helps in an accurate understanding of their condition and lays the foundation for an accurate differential diagnosis and the best course of treatment.
  • The patient is entitled to be clearly informed of the diagnosis and to understand the proposed medical management. The physician proceeds only with the patient's informed consent.
  • Proper diagnosis is followed by treatment that is systematically planned and documented to ensure safety and efficacy.
  • At AyurRemedy, patient safety is a key deliverable throughout the treatment cycle and a key element of process design.
  • Outcomes are tracked scientifically through the treatment cycle with the help of internationally recognised outcome scales.

  1. Evaluation

    History, lifestyle and head-to-toe examination.

  2. Diagnosis

    Explained clearly; nothing starts without your consent.

  3. Treatment

    Planned, documented and carried out safely.

  4. Review

    Outcomes tracked and the plan adjusted.

Treatments may include Panchakarma, other classical treatments and para-surgical procedures.
