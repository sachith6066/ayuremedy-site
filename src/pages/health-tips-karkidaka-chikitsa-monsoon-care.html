Karkidaka Chikitsa: Ayurvedic care for the monsoon

Karkidakam, the peak monsoon month from mid-July to mid-August, is traditionally a time to rest, rebuild strength and prepare the body for the year ahead.

Watch the video

Why the monsoon needs extra care

In Ayurveda, the rainy season (Varsha ritu) is a time when digestion (Agni) naturally weakens and Vata is aggravated by the damp, cool weather. People often notice more joint pain, indigestion, colds and tiredness. The Malayalam month of Karkidakam falls at the height of the monsoon, so traditional Kerala Ayurveda sets it aside for rejuvenation.

What Karkidaka Chikitsa includes

Karkidaka Chikitsa is a seasonal programme planned by a physician for each person. Depending on your health it can include:

  • Abhyanga (oil massage) and herbal steam
  • Panchakarma-based cleansing, such as Vasti, where suitable
  • Rejuvenating medicines
  • A light, easily digested diet

Karkidaka kanji

A traditional part of the season is Karkidaka kanji, a medicated rice gruel cooked with a mix of herbs and spices. It is light, warm and easy to digest, which suits the weaker digestion of the monsoon.
