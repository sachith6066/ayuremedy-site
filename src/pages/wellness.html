Wellness

Relaxing, detoxifying and rejuvenating therapies, combined into programmes planned by our physicians.

Wellness generally means a healthy balance of mind, body and spirit that results in an overall feeling of wellbeing. It is a direction of progress toward an ever-higher level of functioning. Wellness programmes help improve life through positive lifestyle changes, and are followed by people recovering from an illness or condition and by those who simply want better health.

Why we need wellness

Today's hectic pace of life, irregular eating and sleeping habits, tough working conditions, a competitive atmosphere at work and lack of time for oneself all lead to stress. Continuous stress predisposes a person to many health problems; from the common cold to cancer, many of today's illnesses are caused or aggravated by it.

We are also exposed to many toxic substances through food, water and air. These accumulate in the body and over time damage body and mind. Sleep, one of the most important pillars of health, is often disturbed or inadequate, leading to loss of energy and vitality, lower immunity and poor repair of body and mind.

These factors speed up ageing, and parts of the body begin to wear earlier than they should. Postural problems from work and lack of exercise add aches and pains in different parts of the body.

Health slowly slides downhill without one noticing it. The good news is that it can be prevented.

Ayurveda offers a comprehensive answer to these problems.
