Ritucharya

Ayurveda follows the seasons

The classical texts divide the year into six ritus, and each one suits different therapies. Your physician will time a Panchakarma course to the season where it helps.

NowJan – Feb

Shishira

Late winter. Warm oil massage and nourishing diet.

NowMar – Apr

Vasanta

Spring. Classical season for Vamana and Nasya to clear Kapha.

NowMay – Jun

Grishma

Summer. Cooling therapies such as Takradhara and Shirodhara.

NowJul – Aug

Varsha

Monsoon. Vata rises; the traditional time for Vasti.

NowSep – Oct

Sharad

Autumn. Classical season for Virechana to clear Pitta.

NowNov – Dec

Hemanta

Early winter. Digestion is strongest; time to rebuild strength.

Ask the physician whether a seasonal Panchakarma course suits you.

Tridosha

Why no two treatment plans are alike

Ayurveda describes the body through three doshas. Everyone is born with their own balance of them, their prakriti, and illness follows when that balance is disturbed. Your plan is built around yours.

VAir + space

Vata

Governs movement, nerves and circulation. When disturbed: pain, stiffness, dryness, poor sleep, anxiety.

PFire + water

Pitta

Governs digestion, metabolism and heat. When disturbed: acidity, inflammation, skin rashes, irritability.

KEarth + water

Kapha

Governs structure, stability and immunity. When disturbed: weight gain, congestion, sluggishness.
