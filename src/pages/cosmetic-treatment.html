Cosmetic Treatment

Herbal skin and hair treatments using classical Ayurvedic preparations.

Herbal face clean-up

Removes whiteheads, blackheads and blemishes from the facial skin. Regular herbal clean-ups leave the skin glowing and healthy.

Herbal facial

Improves skin colour and the tone of the facial muscles, removes discolouration from sun tan and sun allergy, and improves circulation in the face.

Under-eye pack

Relieves stress and dark circles around the eyes and improves the tone of the eye muscles. It also cools the eyes and reduces tension.

Beauty pack

A full-body scrub followed by body massage and a skin-friendly herbal paste. Removes tanning and improves complexion.

Foot massage

Foot cleaning, foot massage and a foot pack to improve circulation, relieve stress and prevent cracked heels.

Dandruff treatment

Hot medicated anti-dandruff oil applied to the entire scalp, followed by head massage and an anti-dandruff hair pack. Regular treatment helps prevent the return of dandruff, hair fall and early greying.

Ayurvedic hair spa

Head massage and strokes followed by a herbal hair cream. Improves hair texture and quality and promotes hair growth.

Herbal henna

A paste of Ayurvedic herbs applied to the hair from roots to tips. Darkens the hair without scalp allergy.
