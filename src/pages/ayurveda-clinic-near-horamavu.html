Questions patients ask

How far is AyurRemedy from Horamavu?

About 2.4 km, roughly 7 minutes by car in usual traffic via Jayanti Nagar – Thambuchettypalya Main Road and Kowdenahalli Main Road / TC Palya Main Road. Times vary with traffic, so check Google Maps before you leave.

Do I need an appointment?

Booking ahead is best so the doctor you want to see is free. Call 080-4854 2888, WhatsApp +91 94481 50492 or use the booking form, and the clinic will confirm a time.

Can I buy my Ayurvedic medicines at the clinic?

Yes. AyurRemedy has its own Ayurvedic pharmacy under the same roof, open Monday to Saturday 9 am to 8 pm and Sunday 9 am to 1 pm.

Are the doctors available on Sunday?

The clinic and pharmacy are open on Sunday from 9 am to 1 pm, but the doctors do not hold consultations on Sundays. Please book a weekday or Saturday slot to see a doctor.
