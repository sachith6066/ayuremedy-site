Your first consultation

What to expect at your first visit

The consultation fee is ₹500 (online video consultation ₹600). The first consultation is unhurried, so allow some extra time.

  1. Come prepared

    Arrive 10 minutes early. Bring previous reports, scans and prescriptions, and a list of every medicine and supplement you take.

  2. History and examination

    Your physician asks about your health, diet, sleep and routine, examines you, and assesses your body type (prakriti), including pulse examination.

  3. Your plan, explained

    You get a clear explanation of the condition and a plan: medicines from our in-house pharmacy, therapies if needed, and diet and routine advice. Nothing starts without your consent.

  4. Follow-up

    Most conditions need follow-up visits to review progress and adjust the plan. Keep taking any medicines from your other doctors unless they tell you otherwise.
