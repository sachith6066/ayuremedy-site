Frequently Asked Questions

What are the facilities in AyurRemedy?

AyurRemedy is an Ayurvedic clinic with a treatment centre and pharmacy. It offers out-patient consultations, comprehensive Panchakarma, other classical Ayurveda treatments, a Kshara Sutra procedure room and a full Ayurvedic pharmacy. See the image gallery.

Dr Rasmi Johnson holds a BAMS degree and a Master of Surgery (Shalya Tantra), with 23 years of experience. Full profile.

What is the profile of Dr Harshitha?

Dr Harshitha Rai (Harshitha Kumari) holds a BAMS degree, a Doctor of Medicine degree, MD (Dravyaguna), and a PhD, with 13 years of experience. Full profile.

How can I book an appointment?

Go to the Book Appointment page, choose the doctor, date and time you prefer, fill in your details and send the request. It opens WhatsApp with your details ready to send to the clinic.

Will I be notified of appointment confirmation?

Yes. You will receive a call to confirm your appointment on the number you gave when booking.
