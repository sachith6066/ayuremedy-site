Swarnabindu Prashana

Make them stronger, healthier and happier.

Uses

As described in Ayurveda, Swarnabindu Prashana is given to:

  • Boost immunity and help protect your child from infections
  • Support memory and intellect (medha)
  • Improve the overall health of your child
  • Enhance complexion
  • Increase physical and mental strength

Modern life brings stress, altered food habits, distraction and changed farming practices. These affect people's strength, both physically and mentally, and make them more prone to disease. To thrive, a child needs the best of health.

Ayurveda describes health-promoting agents under Rasayana. Swarnabindu Prashana is one such rasayana, described to promote immunity and memory in children.

Swarna (gold) is described as Medhya Vardhana (improving memory), Agni Vardhana (improving metabolism), Bala Vardhana (improving strength), Vrushya (nourishing) and Ayushkara (promoting longevity).

Vaccines protect against specific diseases. Swarnaprashana is described as non-specific, supporting general immunity. It complements your child's routine vaccinations and is not a substitute for any of them.

According to the classical texts, Swarnaprashana increases Medha (intelligence), Agni (digestive capacity), Bala (immunity) and Ayushya (longevity). It is also considered Mangala (auspicious) and Punya.

Why on Pushya Nakshatra?

Pushya Nakshatra has great importance in Ayurveda and is considered a very auspicious day. Medicines collected and taken on this day are said to be especially effective, and the body is believed to absorb them best.

Because Swarnaprashana is given to children to support immunity and intelligence, a prime concern of many parents, it is traditionally given on this auspicious day, once a month.
