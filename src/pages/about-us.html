What is Ayurveda?

Ayurveda is formed from two words, Ayu and Veda: the science explaining life, or as it is generally understood, the science of life. Ayurveda is the ancient system of medicine propagated in India about 5,000 years ago. Its knowledge was passed down orally and later set out in texts by the sages.

Motto of Ayurveda

  • Swasthasya swasthya rakshanam: to preserve the health of a healthy person.
  • Aturasya vikara prashamanam: to alleviate the disease of a diseased person.

Core principles and concepts

  • Panchamahabhuta: the five elements, Prithvi, Jala, Agni, Vayu and Akasha.
  • Tridosha: Vata, Pitta and Kapha, formed from the Panchamahabhuta.

All living beings and non-living things, the entire universe, are composed of the Panchamahabhuta. In the body these form the Tridosha. Since the body and the universe are made of the same five elements, a balance exists between living beings and the universe. When this balance is maintained from time to time and season to season, physical, mental and spiritual health is preserved. When it is lost, the doshas leave their normal state, leading to various kinds of disease.

  • Prakriti: each person's individuality is based on the dosha predominant at birth. This forms their prakriti, which may be Vata, Pitta or Kapha, and the qualities of that dosha shape body and mind. Hence each individual needs a customised routine to keep the doshas in balance.
  • Chikitsa: treatment, divided into Shodhana and Shamana, purificatory and palliative therapy respectively.
  • Panchakarma therapy (the Shodhana therapy) includes procedures that eliminate the vitiated doshas from the body, for example vamana (emesis), virechana (purgation), vasti (enema) and nasya (nasal medication). Panchakarma treatments.
  • Shamana comprises techniques that pacify the doshas and bring them back to normal, for example deepana (carminatives), pachana (digestives), upavasa (fasting) and classical procedures such as sudation with medicated powders or leaves, powder massage (udwarthana) and shirodhara. Other classical treatments.
