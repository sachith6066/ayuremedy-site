Rehabilitation

Specialised care to improve, maintain or restore physical strength, cognition and mobility after illness, injury or surgery.

Rehabilitation describes specialised healthcare dedicated to improving, maintaining or restoring physical strength, cognition and mobility. Typically, rehabilitation helps people gain greater independence after illness, injury or surgery. It can also be described as the process of helping an individual achieve the highest level of function, independence and quality of life possible. Rehabilitation does not reverse the damage caused by disease or trauma; it helps restore the individual to optimal health, functioning and wellbeing.

Stroke Rehabilitation

Paralysis is one of the most common disabilities after a stroke. It usually affects the side of the body opposite the side of the brain damaged by the stroke, and may involve the face, an arm, a leg, or the entire side of the body. This one-sided paralysis is called hemiplegia when there is complete inability to move, or hemiparesis when the weakness is less than total. In Ayurveda, stroke paralysis is known as Pakshaghata (paralysis of one side of the body).

At AyurRemedy, our senior physicians form an optimised treatment plan considering the stage of the paralysis, how long the patient has had it, and their strength. If the paralysis is recent, medication is prescribed along with mild treatments such as Abhyanga (application of medicated oil on the body) or Kaya Seka (pouring of warm medicated oil over the body). A patient who has had paralysis for more than a year needs more extensive treatment, because half of the body has been inactive for so long that the muscles begin to waste.

Cancer Rehabilitation

Our Cancer Rehabilitation Programme is built on a foundation of classical Ayurveda management for patients with primary as well as secondary (metastatic) cancers. It focuses on palliative care to improve the patient's quality of life.

Classical Ayurveda therapies are integrated with the patient's primary cancer treatment to ease the acute side effects of chemotherapy and radiotherapy and to improve functional health and overall wellbeing. The motivation of the patient and their family is supported, which helps patients stay with their treatment. This is an individual programme with a customised diet, lifestyle, medicine and treatment prescription. It supports, and does not replace, treatment by the patient's oncologist.

If you have a history of cancer in your family, you can assess your current risk and follow a protective diet and lifestyle, with the support of classical Ayurvedic herbal medicines, through our Cancer Risk Prevention Programme.
