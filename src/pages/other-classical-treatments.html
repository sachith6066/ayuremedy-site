Classical Ayurvedic therapies

Therapies that relieve pain, stiffness and inflammation, calm the mind and nourish the body, prescribed by the physician for your condition and constitution.

AbhyangamTherapeutic oil massage

A gentle but firm therapeutic oil massage in which two therapists work in synchrony. Oils prepared with different combinations of herbs are chosen according to the person's body nature (prakriti) or health condition, warmed and massaged into the skin. Regular Abhyanga delays ageing, removes fatigue, pacifies Vata, improves eyesight, promotes longevity and sleep, builds resistance to disease and injury and helps quick recovery, improves the complexion and lustre of the skin, relieves Vata and Kapha ailments and increases physical strength.

AgnikarmaThermal cautery

A para-surgical procedure in which heat is applied to painful points using a shalaka, an instrument made of panchadhatu. Agnikarma is indicated in disorders of Asthi (bone), Sandhi (joint) and Snayu (ligament and tendon), and in Arsha (piles), Arbuda (tumour), Bhagandara (fistula-in-ano), ligament injury, tendonitis, joint pain and Gridhrasi (sciatica). It is effective in both acute and chronic pain. Para surgical procedures.

BandhaBandaging

Bandaging any part of the body is called Bandha, with different names by site. A bandage holds the applied medicine in place, prevents excess movement of the affected part, stops bleeding, stops the spread of poison in bites and supports the affected structures.

Choorna pinda swedamPodi kizhi · herbal powder bolus

Choorna (podi) means powder of medicinal plants, pinda (kizhi) means bolus and sweda means fomentation. Fomentation with boluses of medicinal powder reduces pain, inflammation and muscle stiffness. Indicated in swelling, rheumatoid arthritis, muscle spasm, cervical and lumbar spondylitis, paralysis, sprains and cramps.

Dhanya pinda swedamDhanya kizhi · Muthira kizhi

Fomentation with a bolus (potali) containing medicinal grains and seeds. The bolus is touched briefly and repeatedly over each part of the body so that heat passes into the body through the grains. Indicated in arthritis and painful involuntary muscle contractions.

Dhanyamla dharaKaadi dhara

A fermented liquid made from cereals and medicinal herbs is poured over the body in monitored streams for a set time. Widely used for inflammatory diseases such as rheumatoid arthritis and for Vata disorders associated with Kapha or ama (toxins from faulty metabolism). It relieves pain, stiffness and swelling of arthritis and other painful conditions. Indicated in musculoskeletal, neurological and neuromuscular disorders.

Dhanyamla pinda swedamDhanyamla kizhi

Boluses of medicinal powder are dipped in fermented grain liquid and applied over the body in monitored strokes for a set time. Used for inflammatory diseases such as rheumatoid arthritis and Vata disorders with Kapha or ama. Indications include musculoskeletal disorders, inflammatory conditions, and disorders of Vata, Kapha, Vata-Kapha and Ama.

DhoomapanamMedicated smoke

Part of Dinacharya (daily regimen). Herbs or herbal powders are burnt and the smoke inhaled, keeping the head, respiratory system and sense organs clean and healthy. Ideal for bronchial asthma, sinusitis and upper and lower respiratory infections. It quickly clears vitiated Kapha from the head, dries excess mucus, reduces inflammation of the sinuses and airways, and helps prevent diseases above the shoulders arising from Kapha and Vata.

GandooshaOil pulling

The mouth is filled to capacity with medicated decoction or oil, held for some time and then spat out. Good for diseases of the ears, mouth, throat and eyes, excess salivation, nausea, loss of taste, facial paralysis, neck stiffness, head disorders and stomatitis. It improves the voice and complexion and helps prevent diseases of the mouth, lips and teeth.

Greeva vastiNeck oil pooling

Medicated oil is held over the cervical region in a ring of black-gram dough for a set time, replaced with warm oil as it cools. It reduces pain and inflammation, helps cervical degeneration, increases the flexibility of the neck joints and strengthens the neck muscles. Indicated in cervical spondylitis, ankylosing spondylitis, fibromyalgia and neck stiffness.

Kati vastiLower back oil pooling

Medicated oil is held over the lower back for about 30–45 minutes inside a ring of black-gram paste, with cooled oil replaced by warm oil to keep the temperature steady. It strengthens the lower back muscles, reduces inflammation of the lumbar nerves, increases the flexibility of the lumbar spine and helps relieve nerve compression where the disc space is reduced. Indicated in low back pain, spondylitis, spondylosis, sciatica, spinal displacement, muscle sprain, fibromyalgia, scoliosis and lordosis.

KayasekaWarm oil stream on the body

Warm medicated oil is poured from a set height continuously over the whole body for a set time. Best indicated in neuromuscular disorders, fractures, joint dislocation, peripheral neuropathy and sprains.

LepamMedicated paste

Medicated paste is applied to a part of the body and washed off with warm water once dry. It reduces local inflammation, improves muscle strength and tone, and improves skin colour and tone. Indicated in sprains, swelling, pain, discolouration, skin diseases, tendonitis, myofascial pain and carpal tunnel syndrome.

Mukha lepamFace pack

A thick medicated paste is applied to the face and removed with warm water once dry. It improves the colour and lustre of the face, removes discolouration and tan, and makes skin smooth and soft. Indicated in acne, white patches, dry skin, dark discolouration, bacterial infections and scars.

Netra prakshalanamEye wash

Medicated decoction is poured into the eye from the inner corner to flow towards the outer corner, cleaning the eye. It removes dead cells, dirt, dust and toxins, clears accumulated Kapha and keeps the white of the eye clear. Indicated in myopia, hypermetropia, conjunctivitis, cataract, stye, blepharitis, hordeolum, meibomitis and dacryocystitis.

Netra tarpanamGhee held over the eyes

Early in the morning or evening, the patient lies flat and a 3 cm wall of barley and black-gram dough is built around the eyes. With the eyes closed, liquefied medicated ghee or oil is poured up to the eyelashes, and the patient then opens the eyes slowly. It improves clarity of vision and the tone of the eye muscles, supports the optic and trigeminal nerves, cleans the eyes and relieves strain.

PichuWarm oil pad

A cotton pad soaked in warm medicated oil is placed on the affected part and kept warm. It reduces local inflammation and strengthens muscles and tendons. Indicated in frozen shoulder, carpal tunnel syndrome, tendonitis, fracture and dislocation, myofascial pain, sprain, tennis elbow, golfer's elbow, bursitis and local stress.

Patra pinda swedaElakizhi · leaf bolus

Effective for many kinds of arthritis, spondylitis, back pain, sports injuries and other soft-tissue inflammation, especially joint pain. Fresh leaves of anti-Vata plants such as Eranda (castor), Arka (Calotropis), Nirgundi (Vitex negundo), Rasna, coconut leaves, lemon and turmeric are fried with herbal ingredients, tied into cloth boluses, dipped in warm medicated oil and used to massage the body. It improves circulation in the area and induces sweating, which helps the skin eliminate wastes.

ShirodharaOil stream on the forehead

A classical, well-established Ayurvedic procedure in which medicated oil or other liquids drip slowly and steadily onto the forehead. It induces a relaxed state of awareness that brings a dynamic balance of mind and body.

ShirovastiOil held on the head

A band is tied around the head and an open-topped cap fixed in place. Medicated oil warmed to body temperature is poured into the cap and held on the scalp for about 30–45 minutes, depending on the person's constitution. Ideal for insomnia, Bell's palsy, neurological and mental disorders, headache and stress.

Shiro lepaThalapothichil

Medicated paste is smeared over the head, which is then wrapped with specific leaves and kept for 30–45 minutes depending on the patient's condition. A comprehensive treatment for disorders of the central nervous system; it also improves memory and concentration, relieves stress and tension, promotes sleep and reduces hair fall. Indicated in paralysis, sleep disorders, psychiatric and nervous disorders, autism, ADHD, dandruff, Parkinsonism, Alzheimer's disease and facial paralysis.

Shashtika shali pinda swedaNjavarakizhi · rice bolus

Medicated oil is applied generously over the whole body, which is then massaged with small cloth boluses of cooked Njavara rice dipped in a mixture of cow's milk and herbal decoction, inducing sweating. Highly rejuvenating and nourishing, it builds the body, strengthens the nervous system and improves the skin.

TakradharaMedicated buttermilk stream

Similar to Shirodhara: takra means buttermilk and dhara means pouring. Medicated buttermilk is poured onto the forehead. Used for chronic insomnia, psoriasis and other Pitta disorders.

UdwarthanamHerbal powder massage

A therapeutic deep-tissue massage or Ayurvedic body scrub using herbal powder (choornam) instead of oil, for 30–45 minutes. Used in obesity, diabetic neuropathy, paralysis, skin care, sciatica and hardening of the blood vessels, among others.

Valuka swedamSand bolus

Valuka means purified sand. Fomentation with a bolus of processed sand, used in rheumatoid arthritis, muscle spasm, back pain, muscle sprain and stiffness.
