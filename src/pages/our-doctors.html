Our Doctors

Our team includes MD (Ayu) and PhD qualified physicians from leading institutions of higher learning in Ayurveda, supported by trained Ayurveda therapists and nurses.

BAMS · MS (Shalya Tantra)

Dr Rasmi Johnson

Dr Rasmi Johnson, senior physician at AyurRemedy, brings 23 years of experience. She holds a BAMS degree from Vaidyaratnam Ayurveda College, Thrissur, Kerala, and a Master's in Ayurveda from SDM Ayurveda College, Hassan. She has worked under Vaidyabhushanam Raghavan Thirumulpad and Dr V V Peter, Chief Physician, Kottakkal Arya Vaidya Sala Agency, Mysore. She worked as faculty at Aswini Ayurveda College, Tumkur, and as Head of Department at Coimbatore Ayurveda College, Coimbatore. Before AyurRemedy, she was senior physician at AyurVaid Hospital, Ramamurthy Nagar.

She specialises in para-surgical procedures: Kshara Karma (caustic cautery), Kshara Sutra (caustic thread), Agnikarma (thermal cautery) and Raktamokshana (bloodletting) for various surgical disorders. She is also experienced in treating diabetes and diabetic complications, infertility, PCOD, and joint disorders such as sciatica, cervical and lumbar spondylosis, osteoarthritis and rheumatoid arthritis.

  • Kshara Sutra
  • Kshara Karma
  • Agnikarma
  • Raktamokshana
  • Diabetic complications
  • Infertility & PCOD
  • Sciatica & spondylosis
  • Spondylitis
  • Arthritis
  • Skin diseases

Dr Rasmi Johnson has participated in clinical studies and research and presented papers at many national and international conferences:

  • "Concept of Rasayana Chikitsa", Global Summit international seminar, Vaidyaratnam Ayurveda College and Hospital, Thrissur, Kerala
  • "Ayurvedic approach to the diagnosis of Jalodara", Symposium on Udara Chikitsa
  • "Effect of Pratisaraniya Kshara in the management of Gudabhramsha with special reference to rectal prolapse", national seminar on Arsha, SDMCAH, Hassan
  • "Sroto Sharira", state-level seminar organised by the All India Shareera Research Institute, Karnataka

BAMS · MD · PhD (Ayu)

Dr Harshitha Rai

Dr Harshitha Rai (Harshitha Kumari), senior physician at AyurRemedy, brings 13 years of experience. She completed her BAMS at KVG Ayurveda Medical College, Sullia, her postgraduation at SDM Ayurveda College, Udupi, and her PhD at IPGT & RA, Gujarat Ayurved University, Jamnagar. She has worked as faculty at Alva's Ayurveda Medical College and Hospital, Moodbidri, and as a research associate at I-AIM, Bengaluru, where she conducted extensive research into new herbs to treat lifestyle diseases.

She is experienced in treating joint disorders such as osteoarthritis and rheumatoid arthritis, skin diseases, and lifestyle disorders such as obesity, hypothyroidism and PCOD.

  • Osteoarthritis
  • Rheumatoid arthritis
  • Skin diseases
  • Obesity
  • Hypothyroidism
  • PCOD

Dr Harshitha has participated in clinical studies and research, has several publications to her name, and continues to publish and lecture regularly on Ayurveda and health.
